Sim-to-real manipulation with Gaussian-splat simulation

PhD research · UR5 · MuJoCo-Warp · Gaussian splatting · 2026

I train camera policies in simulation and run them on a real UR5 with an OnRobot RG2 gripper and wrist cameras. The simulator is MuJoCo-Warp, and the camera images are rendered from a Gaussian-splat scan of the lab. No real robot data is used for training.

21 / 29 picks on the real UR5 with a policy trained only in simulation98.9% success over 5,120 simulated episodes6.5× higher training throughput per GPU

Stereo-inertial SLAM and monocular VIO

Jetson Nano · RealSense D455 · GTSAM · Basalt · 2026

AnchorFlow-VIO is an open-source monocular visual-inertial odometry system based on Basalt. I evaluated it on the LaMAria benchmark, and it runs at 84 frames per second on a desktop CPU. I also built a stereo-inertial SLAM system for a small mobile robot with a Jetson Nano 4 GB. It has loop closure, dense mapping and relocalisation.

0.17 m loop drift with loop closure8 ms tracking delay on the Jetson Nano84 fps AnchorFlow-VIO on a desktop CPU

Continual learning for manipulation

PhD thesis · mjlab · reinforcement learning · 2023 – present

My thesis is about adding new manipulation skills to one policy without storing the data of earlier tasks. I built a benchmark of 37 manipulation tasks and trained a separate teacher policy for each task. The teachers are then combined into one network one after another.

37 manipulation tasks in the benchmark87.6% average success over 15 tasks with one network4 tasks run by one policy on a real 7-DOF arm

Custom quadruped robot

with L Square Labs · CAD · 3D printing · MuJoCo · RL · 2025 – present

I designed, 3D-printed and assembled a 12-joint quadruped. The walking policy is trained with reinforcement learning in MuJoCo and transferred to the robot. The robot walks untethered at 0.3 m/s and did not fall in the recorded trials.

Manipulation control and teleoperation

Kinova Gen3 · xArm6 · Franka · UR5 · RealSense / ZED · Meta Quest 3 · 2024 – 2026

I wrote control and perception software for an xArm6 (250 Hz) and a Kinova Gen3 (1 kHz torque control). It is operated from a browser and has five controllers, including gravity compensation and Cartesian impedance control. It also has four metric depth models and SAM2 segmentation. I also built VR teleoperation with a Meta Quest 3 for collecting demonstrations.

1 kHz torque control on the Kinova Gen35 controllers, including Cartesian impedance4 metric depth models
