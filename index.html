Sim-to-real manipulation with Gaussian-splat simulation
2026
PhD research · UR5 · MuJoCo-Warp · Gaussian splatting

I train camera policies in simulation and run them on a real UR5 with an OnRobot RG2 gripper and wrist cameras. The simulator is MuJoCo-Warp, and the camera images are rendered from a Gaussian-splat scan of the lab. No real robot data is used for training.

  • An RGB-only policy picked the bottle in 21 of 29 attempts on the real robot (72%).
  • A policy that uses camera point clouds reached 98.9% success over 5,120 simulated episodes.
  • Training runs with 21,504 parallel environments on 7 A100 GPUs. Throughput per GPU is 6.5 times higher than in the first version.
  • With FastTD3, the peg-in-hole teacher policy trains in about 30 minutes, compared with about 8 hours with PPO.

Stereo-inertial SLAM and monocular VIO
2026
Jetson Nano · RealSense D455 · GTSAM · Basalt

AnchorFlow-VIO is an open-source monocular visual-inertial odometry system based on Basalt. I evaluated it on the LaMAria benchmark, and it runs at 84 frames per second on a desktop CPU. I also built a stereo-inertial SLAM system for a small mobile robot with a Jetson Nano 4 GB. It has loop closure, dense mapping and relocalisation.

  • On a test loop, drift was reduced from 1.47 m with odometry alone to 0.17 m with loop closure. ORB-SLAM3 gave 0.14 to 0.17 m on the same data.
  • Tracking delay on the Jetson Nano was reduced from 78 ms to 8 ms.
Mapping one floor of a building with loop closure (4 times faster).

Continual learning for manipulation
2023 – present
PhD thesis · mjlab · reinforcement learning

My thesis is about adding new manipulation skills to one policy without storing the data of earlier tasks. I built a benchmark of 37 manipulation tasks and trained a separate teacher policy for each task. The teachers are then combined into one network one after another.

  • One network reached 87.6% average success over 15 tasks. The baseline without consolidation reached 79.5%.
  • A single combined policy performed four tasks on a real 7-DOF arm without fine-tuning on the hardware.

Manipulation control and teleoperation
2024 – 2026
Kinova Gen3 · xArm6 · Franka · UR5 · RealSense / ZED · Meta Quest 3
