Details

Desert simulation with 3D LiDAR

As a Junior Research Fellow at IIT Jodhpur in 2023 I built a Unity simulation of a desert environment for collecting sensor data. I wrote a 3D LiDAR sensor model for Unity that publishes point clouds to ROS. The LiDAR package is public on GitHub.

Deformable objects

Environments in Isaac Sim for deformable objects such as cables and plants, used for testing manipulation.

Mechanisms in MuJoCo

I converted a six-axis parallel robot from a CAD assembly into a MuJoCo model with closed kinematic chains. I also modelled a two-axis handle driven by quasi-direct-drive actuators.

Quadcopter

Trajectory tracking with a quadcopter in a motion-capture room, from my earlier work on aerial robots.
